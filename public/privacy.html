What this form asks for

If and when the interest form is enabled, it asks for an email address, optional name, a traveler's destination or a guide's home city/region, and languages (optional for travelers; required for guides). The service records the chosen interest type, consent-notice version and timestamp, creation time, and status. For abuse prevention it stores a keyed HMAC hash of the request IP rather than the raw IP in the application database. Cloudflare may separately process request IP and technical metadata for delivery and security; verify its current logging, service-specific terms, subprocessors, and retention before enabling the form.

It does not ask for a phone number, government ID, identity document, OTP, payment information, precise location, or itinerary details.

Why and how information is used

Information would be used only to reply about launch interest or guide onboarding, based on the consent shown beside the form. It is not a trip booking, account registration, or marketing subscription. We do not sell interest-list data. No message should contain sensitive or identity information.
